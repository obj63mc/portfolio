# Single zoom level with world-pixel coordinates

Every scene has one zoom level and one coordinate system, world pixels, on every device. Devices differ only in a render scale fixed for the session (1.0 desktop, about 0.6 phone), never in what a world pixel means. We chose this over pinch or wheel zoom because cursor positions are broadcast to every visitor in the room and must mean the same thing on every screen, and because one camera model (edge-push, drag, joystick) then works unchanged across desktop, touch, overworld and sub-scenes. The cost is that phones see a keyhole into the scene and must pan more; that is accepted and mitigated by the wider push band and the small overworld.

**Amendment (Joe, 2026-09-29, buildout ticket 17):** one exception. While the Foundry screen plays a reel, a visitor whose own cursor is in the theatre's seats (or who is sitting down for a reel they started) sees the camera ease out to frame the projector and the whole screen, at whatever scale fits the viewport and never closer than the session's; it eases back when the reel ends or they leave the seats. Everyone else in the room, one arriving mid-reel included, keeps the default view, so each visitor's zoom is their own, as at the movies. World pixels still mean the same thing everywhere, so cursors on the wire are unaffected, and cursors keep their on-screen size. It is the only zoom: there is still no pinch or wheel zoom.
