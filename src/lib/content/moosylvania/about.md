This scene is the lobby of Moosylvania, the agency I currently work for. I joined as a Senior Web Developer in August 2011 and have been its Chief Architect since August 2013, running the Interactive Department.

There I own the architecture, stack and hosting for all of the agency's web work. I ship five or six new websites a year from estimate to launch, personally run the hosting for about 45 websites and applications across AWS, Cloudflare, Heroku Render and Convex; lead a development team that has ranged from one developer to ten, alongside the agency's writers and creatives.

The four computers in the lobby are the stacks I work in, each with the tools I reach for: the front end (Vue and Nuxt, TypeScript, Svelte, React), the back end and hosting (Node.js, PHP, Python, Docker and the cloud providers), the content and marketing systems (Silverstripe, Shopify, WordPress, Strapi, HubSpot, Salesforce Marketing Cloud and more) and the data behind them (MySQL, PostgreSQL, Redis, MongoDB, Elasticsearch). The meeting TV and its remote are shared with everyone visiting the lobby at the same time, and the moose is the agency's own.

The client work itself is in the scenes nearby: spirits and beer at Side Project Cellar, cigars at Brennan's, PayPal, Venmo and Panera at St. Louis Bread Co., and film promotions at The Foundry.
