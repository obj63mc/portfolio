This scene is a theater at the Alamo Drafthouse in City Foundry. In the scene I am referencing the films we worked on in my first years at Moosylvania, as a Senior Web Developer from 2011 to 2013.

The work consisted of building promotions and games for Universal Pictures Home Entertainment's home-video releases. The scene contains three posters on the wall: each poster starts its film's reel on the big screen, which everyone visiting the theatre at the same time watches together. Examples of the films we worked on were Fast Five, The Lorax, and Snow White and the Hunstsman.
