This scene is an illustration of a computer science lab in McDonnell Douglas Hall at Saint Louis University, where I earned my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science, with honors, from 2001 to 2005.

## Scene Props
- **The diploma** is the degree itself.
- **The whiteboard** is my senior project: a static analyzer that built control-flow graphs of C and Java source and computed cyclomatic complexity for basis path testing.
- **The lab workstation** is my GitHub, where you can see what I am working on now, including how this site works.

While finishing the degree I started on the help desk at MonsterCommerce in Belleville, which is where the rest of the story picks up.
