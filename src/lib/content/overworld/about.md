Welcome to my portfolio, illustrated as the St. Louis Region allowing you to creatively explore and learn more about me. Each district in the map holds a place from my career or my life, and each place opens onto a scene with examples of my work. This page you are viewing is the same content without the animation and is available for accessibility and users with Javascript disabled. Everything below is what you would find by exploring.

## Scenes
- **Maplewood** is where I work. Moosylvania is the agency where I started as a Senior Web Developer in 2011 and have been Chief Architect since 2013; inside are the front-end, back-end, CMS and data stacks I use. Across the parking lot, Side Project Cellar holds the beer and spirits brands I have built websites for, and St. Louis Bread Co. holds my work for PayPal, Venmo and Panera.
- **Central West End** has Brennan's, with the cigar brand websites I build and manage for Scandinavian Tobacco Group.
- **Midtown** has Saint Louis University, where I earned my BS in Computer Science with honors in 2005, and The Foundry, showing the games I built for Universal Pictures Home Entertainment's home-video releases.
- **Belleville**, across the river in Illinois, is where I started in 2004 at MonsterCommerce, an e-commerce platform later acquired by Network Solutions, where I stayed as an engineer until 2011. Its server rack holds screenshots of what I helped build there. The angler on the bank leads to Big Muddy, one of my old agency games rebuilt for this site.
- **Forest Park** has Sushi Stand, the other agency game I rebuilt, behind the koi in the Grand Basin.
- **Carondelet Park** let's you learn a bit more about me: I used to race for South Side Cyclery, and there is a lap track to ride against the clock.
