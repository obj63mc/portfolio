New composition, 2026-10-01: the St. Louis Bread Co. café (scene bread-co), a sixth interior, for the PayPal, Venmo and UX-testing work Joe did at Moosylvania. Six Codex drafts (codex exec, codex-cli 0.159.2, its built-in image tool; each the tool's native 1672 x 941 output, unresized; kept under art/references/local/drafts/bread-co-<a..f>.png). Drafts a, b and c came from the first prompt below, with the three interior photographs of the Chippewa Street café and Brennan's approved master for rendering and scale. All three cut the laptop table's pedestal and chairs off at the bottom edge; in a and c the laptop's screen met the counter's base; in b a plant on the left table covered the ATM's foot; in c the left table was cut by the left edge. Draft a had the cleanest door, ATM, counter and scan-to-pay stand, so drafts d, e and f redrew it from the second prompt below, a fix list with draft a attached as the composition: both tables and their chairs a fifth smaller, at Brennan's scale; the laptop table whole inside the image with carpet below its feet and a band of floor between the laptop's screen and the counter; the other table moved away from the door. Draft e is the approved composition: the widest band between the laptop and the counter, the most carpet under the laptop table's feet, and open floor in front of both the door and the ATM (in d and f the left chair stands in front of the door). It added a small potted sprig on the left table, kept.
