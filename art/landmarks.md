[Cursor Camp](https://neal.fun/cursor-camp/) was inspected in the user's real Chrome session after automated Chrome hit a verification page. Observed: broad connected paths, consistent elevated perspective, buildings and props with readable silhouettes, foreground trees and shared ground details. Use those composition principles; this project retains its separate outline-free Moosylvania style. The older [mechanics research](../docs/research/cursor-camp-mechanics.md) remains useful but explicitly labels its inferences.

Original style references are listed in [the reference index](../prototypes/art-pipeline/reference/INDEX.md). `ref-scene-0-arrival-mock.png` carries architecture; `ref-scene-1-day.png` carries palette; the theatre and office references carry room shape language. Avoid copying the original lettering into generated props: accessible text and brand-specific content are supplied by the application.
