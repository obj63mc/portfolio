## Overworld geography

Viewed [Arch-area satellite imagery](https://www.google.com/maps/@38.6247,-90.1866,1548m/data=!3m1!1e3) and [metro-area satellite imagery](https://www.google.com/maps/@38.625,-90.255,14600m/data=!3m1!1e3). The retained composition uses a compressed illustrated map:

- Mississippi on the east; the Arch stands on its own riverside lawn. I-44 runs **west, beside the Arch grounds**, not through the Arch.
- Eads Bridge is north of the Arch; the larger I-64/Poplar Street crossing is south. These are distinct crossings.
- Maplewood is southwest, Forest Park northwest, Central West End northeast of Forest Park, SLU/Foundry in Midtown, downtown east and Carondelet Park south. Distances and individual street grids are stylized.
- Maplewood has no separate scene. Church entrance, welcome board, signpost and moose are placed on its overworld district. The rider belongs to the southern park district. No historical MonsterCommerce office facade has been verified; the Belleville warehouse is invented, and only its rooftop sign follows a verified source: Joe's MonsterCommerce logo and purple monster mascot (`references/local/places/monstercommerce/`, owner-supplied, logged in `references/locations.json`).

The overworld master is a diorama, not an aerial map: the camera sits at about 35 degrees, the skyline band along the top stands in for downtown, and only the named landmarks are drawn at cursor scale. Its river polygon, Eads deck, Poplar Street south end and Arch reset point in `src/lib/scenes/overworld.ts` were measured on that master.

## Visual language

[Cursor Camp](https://neal.fun/cursor-camp/) informs connected paths, shared ground, consistent camera angle and readable props; one screenshot of it (via Aftermath's write-up, kept in `references/local/cursor-camp/`) is attached to the overworld prompt for camera angle and object scale only. The original [daytime illustration](references/ref-scene-1-day.png) supplies bright greens, cyan, cream and coral. Brown is restricted to photographed wood, brick and upholstery instead of tinting every scene. The [arrival mock](references/ref-scene-0-arrival-mock.png) remains an architecture reference. Accessible labels and portfolio content are supplied by the application.

## Fill scenery between the key scenes

The ground between the districts is St. Louis, not woodland: Forest Park (Art Museum, Grand Basin, Planetarium) north of Maplewood; south-city brick bungalows, Ted Drewes and Bevo Mill south-west of the plaza; Maplewood side streets south of the church; the Barnes-Jewish medical campus and a MetroLink platform between the West End row and SLU; downtown west of the Arch (Union Station and the Wheel, the City Museum, Washington Avenue lofts, an office core, the Old Post Office, Kiener Plaza, Ballpark Village); Laclede's Landing north of the Arch; the Arch grounds as a designed landscape with the Old Cathedral; Chouteau's Landing, Busch Stadium, Soulard and the brewery south of the highway; the Gateway Geyser, a grain elevator and farmland on the Illinois bank; Belleville's main street, cathedral and square. All are background scenery at roughly their real proportion to the Arch; only the five venues and the park are enterable. Photo sources and attributions: `references/locations.json` (`fill/` entries).

The St. Louis Bread Co. east of Ted Drewes (2026-10-01, [review](reviews/2026-10-01-overworld-bread-co.md)) is the café at 6607 Chippewa St, down the street from the real Ted Drewes, drawn after a Google Street View screenshot of its corner supplied by Joe (`references/local/places/st-louis-bread-co/exterior-street-view.png`, a local reference, not committed; logged in `references/locations.json` under `bread-co`). From the photograph: the one-storey tan brick building with a flat roof and pale coping, the dark standing-seam metal canopy wrapping the corner, the arched windows under it, the olive-green sign panels on the parapet, the patio at one end with green umbrellas behind wooden screens, and the pink flowering shrubs along the front. Invented for the map: the glass double door in the middle of the front wall, the café interior's door (the photograph's entrance is out of clear view, beside the patio); the parapet's height, raised to take the sign, the real Saint Louis Bread Co. logo Joe supplied on its sage ground, on the front wall alone; and its place on the map, which follows the illustrated map's compressed geography, not Chippewa Street's.

**Invented fixture, not a landmark:** the angler on the Illinois bank's sand point west of the grain elevator (2026-10-01) is the portfolio's own, the door to Big Muddy; no reference photo stands behind them, and their size follows the drawn cursor, not the elevator.
