# Location-fidelity revision

This review supersedes the first batch and its standalone Maplewood proof. See the paired JSON for all retained source hashes.

The six composed masters were compared with selected actual-location photos, then with the stitched background/prop/foreground composites. The workshop was exercised in Chrome with the 390 × 844 phone frame, 1.25× and 2× tiles, master comparison and reduced-motion/click-reaction controls. The initial overworld signpost fits the phone frame. No browser errors were reported.

- **Overworld:** brighter shared ground and vegetation; Arch on a separate lawn with highway to its west, Eads north, Poplar crossing south. Maplewood, welcome/signpost/moose and church-door/tree layers live in this world. Geography is compressed, not survey-accurate.
- **Moosylvania:** the church studio's white arched windows, exposed dark ceiling, mezzanine, lime seating and pale desks replace a generic brown lobby. Four separately positioned monitors use the existing desk rows. The foreground monitor was reduced to the original footprint; the damaged third monitor was replaced with a measured matte of the original pixels.
- **SLU:** modern McDonnell Douglas Hall lobby and glass-sided classroom entrance, gray sofa, cream walls and blond doors. The rejected extracted chair had a hole in its upholstery; a measured matte now retains the original solid chair and exact floor contact.
- **Foundry/Alamo:** orange acoustic walls and cream sconces from the actual auditorium reference, dark reclining seats and dining tables facing the screen. The seat band follows the room perspective and keeps the side aisle clear.
- **Side Project:** blue-gray walls, blond joists, pale bar, white metal stools and concrete floor. The tap bank rests on the bar, and the extracted stool matches its row.
- **Brennan's:** teal millwork, tin ceiling and globe pendants, caramel banquette, brick/bar wall and glass humidor. The foreground chair was shifted down to match the original rug/floor contact. Its bounding rectangle overlaps the cabinet base naturally; the cabinet's main glass face remains visible. The ATM panel is an inferred portfolio fixture.

Accepted as a coherent workshop draft. The photographs are references, not exact floor-plan measurements. Production routes still use prior artwork/geometry; those integrations belong to issues 04/05. Regenerating a master invalidates its crop and placement assumptions and requires a fresh visual judgment.
