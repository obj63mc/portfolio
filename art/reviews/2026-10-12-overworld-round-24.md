# Overworld round twenty-four: the cream shop south of the moose redrawn

**Verdict:** on the installed master, the assembled scene and the plate's tiles at both densities, the cream two-storey shop with the green awning on the Maplewood road south of the moose is a freshly drawn building on the same footprint: its front faces the viewer and its east wall recedes at the map's angle, its awning is one straight band, a door stands between two shop windows, a round bush stands at each end of the front on a cream pavement, and nothing of the old bicycle rack remains. Every automated check passes. Workshop acceptance in `art/review.html`, and the read on the running site, are Joe's.

Joe (2026-10-12), from a screenshot of the building: its front looked like a broken bike blended into the building, and it should be a normal-looking house. Then, of a first fix: the wall by the awning looked curved and the awning should be straight; a window on the side wall faced the viewer and overhung the bush; the wall's colour differed there; and finally the whole building looked off, its angles wrong from the awning to the side wall to the bushes, so it was to be regenerated from scratch.

- **The defect, native px.** The master painted a rack of bicycles along the shop's front, x 524 to 553, y 526 to 536, under the awning, which the 4× upscale smeared into a tangle over the lower wall. The awning's right end tapered off in a ragged diagonal at the corner (x 553 to 555, y 519 to 524), the wall there read as curved, and the east wing's ground floor carried a dark slanting arch (x 558 to 568, y 516 to 524).
- **Withdrawn first.** A hand tile (`scripts/art/overworld/shop-front.py`, now deleted) painted the bikes out and relaid the front in the base's own colours, then straightened the awning's end and the corner, then replaced the wing's arch with a front-facing window, then with a narrow slit in a wall relaid from the face's own column tones. Joe rejected each read in turn: the awning and corner were still curved, the window faced the wrong way and overhung the bush, the wall's tone differed, and the building's angles were wrong throughout. Patches on a badly drawn building did not add up to a well-drawn one.
- **The round.** `sources/overworld-fix/rounds/round-24.json`, one 256 px tile, `shopfront`, its repaint area the whole building and its bushes, x 519 to 572, y 494 to 540 (the road north, the big tree west, the trees east and the brick house south kept). Codex drew it three times from the fix list (`rounds/codex-24.json`, manifest by `tile-manifest.py`; the runs are under `art/runs/overworld-fix-r24-shopfront*`): all three gave a clean building in the map's camera; b is kept (`tiles/t-shopfront-model.png`), the crispest, with the awning straight, a door in the middle and a bush at each end; a added a third bush at the left and c was softer. The attempts' generated folders and source PNGs were removed after the choice: `src/lib/engine/props.ts` globs `art/generated/overworld/*/asset.json`, and an attempt's asset has no world rect, so the site build fails while they exist.
- **Stitch.** Drift 6.0 outside the mask (the model's whole-tile output, under the 14 limit), no pixel changed outside the mask; `tiles.py check` found 1 dead-end road px in no cluster.
- **Install.** `install-master.sh twenty-four` upscaled the master 4×, composited the six decals, re-derived the plate, its tiles at both densities and the master-derived mattes, listed no stale extraction and validated. `sync-geometry.py` changed nothing. Against the committed master, the new one differs by more than 6 % of a channel only inside 4× px x 1904 to 2322, y 1940 to 2229, the edit. The Maplewood tree matte's registration crop touches the repaint area's west edge, so its halo of plate px changed along that edge (501 px at a 2 % fuzz); the tree itself is the same, and the halo lands on identical plate pixels at rest.
- **Geometry.** Nothing here is a prop, a cut-out, a decal or a hit target; the building is plate scenery between the moose rig's rest rect and the brick house. No rect in `src/lib/scenes/overworld.ts` changed.
- **Read.** At 3× on the installed master, at 4× to 6× on plate tiles 3-3 at both densities, on the composite and on a 4× preview of the stitched native crop: the building whole on its footprint, its roof, parapet, windows, awning, door, bushes and pavement where the fix list put them, the lawn joining the lawn outside the outline, the road and the neighbouring tree, house and trees unchanged, no seam or tone step at the mask's edge.
- **Not read.** The running site: the phone frame, foreground ordering, motion and reduced motion were not looked at; the round touches no prop, rig or foreground layer.
- **Checks.** `art:check`, `art:test`, `art:validate`, `check`, `build` and `npm test` pass.
