# Overworld round sixteen: the SLU logomark on McDonnell Douglas Hall

**Verdict:** the SLU logomark is on McDonnell Douglas Hall's central parapet, crisp at both tile densities, and the 4× master is reinstalled. Every automated check passes. Workshop acceptance in `art/review.html` is Joe's. See [asset hashes and checks](2026-09-29-overworld-round-16.json).

Joe (2026-09-29) asked for the SLU logo on McDonnell Douglas Hall, so that users know it is an SLU campus building, and supplied the blue logomark as PNG and then as SVG.

- **Placement.** The crest sits on the cream front face of the tall central block, centred above the glass atrium entrance. That face is the largest plain wall facing the camera (about 108 × 20 native px). The crest's centre is (872, 287.5) native, (2968, 979) world. It is 18 native px tall (61 world px, 72 px in the 4× master), sheared 0.12 down to the east to follow the parapet's top edge and the atrium's glass line.
- **Why a decal.** Pasted into `stitched.png` at 12 × 18 px, the logo came out of the 4× Real-ESRGAN pass as a blue shield with a white smudge. A hard-edged paste (every pixel snapped to blue or white) was worse. So the logo is a decal: `sources/overworld-fix/decals.json` places `slu-logomark.svg` in native px, and `install-master.sh` composites it onto the 4× source before installing it. The SVG is rasterised by ImageMagick straight at 8× supersampling, sheared in premultiplied alpha and downscaled once. `stitched.png` is unchanged and carries no logo: under a native paste, the upscale left blue specks outside the crisp decal's outline. The master's provenance records the decal file hashes.
- **The logo.** Joe's `slu-logomark-blue-rgb.svg`, with its TM path removed (at this size it would be a speck). Its white outer ring stands slightly brighter than the cream wall, like a crest mounted on the facade. The real facade's signage was not verified; the crest is a wayfinding fixture.
- **Read.** In the 2× and 1.25× tiles (5-1 and 5-2), the cross, crown, rings and fleur-de-lis are all distinct. The crest stays within the face, clear of the roof edge and the atrium canopy. In a 390 × 844 frame at 0.6 centred on SLU, it reads as the SLU shield above the entrance. No prop, hit target or foreground layer is near it, and the geometry sync leaves `src/lib/scenes/overworld.ts` unchanged.
- As in round fifteen, every plate tile and master-derived matte differs from the last install by a few levels, because a changed 4× master is re-quantised as lossy WebP. An unchanged `stitched.png` with unchanged decals still reinstalls byte for byte.
