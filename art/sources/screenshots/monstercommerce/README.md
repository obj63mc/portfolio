## Fidelity and source notes

- All crops and all screenshots placed in comparison canvases were checked against their original RGB pixels. No rescaling, sharpening, repainting or AI reconstruction was applied.
- Exterior blank margins and Wayback toolbar areas were removed from the selected homepage replays. Existing website content, including missing-image areas, remains as archived.
- Application crops remove period browser chrome while preserving the UI. Original training callouts remain. Screens that ended at a viewport boundary in the source remain incomplete at that boundary.
- The Account Manager source already contains black privacy redactions and a red instructional circle. They are preserved.
- Native source sizes vary. Comparison images contain only the screenshots, top aligned with a 24px gap. Titles and version labels remain in the gallery and metadata only. Small historical screenshots have not been enlarged.
- 3.x is supported by the official 2002 manual and explicit v3.0 login figure. 4.x versions are based on original visible footers or the official screenshot series. Most 4.x image captures are April 10, 2006.
- Network Solutions header editing is attributed to the **7.x family**, from a manual with a 2009 copyright. Its exact patch version and screenshot date are unknown.
