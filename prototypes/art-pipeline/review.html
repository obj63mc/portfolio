Maplewood at world scale, with the props on it

The background is stretched to the district footprint width in world px. Drag props to place them; drag the crosshair to the arrival point (the welcome sign). Props are fitted to the world sizes in the controls (world px at a 1400 footprint, keeping each image's aspect) and anchored at their base, so the art's pixel size does not matter. Defaults put the signpost on the sidewalk where the street tree was, the welcome sign on the grass right of the church, and the double door in the church's arched gap (measured at about 95x85 world px), leaving the lawn in front of the church clear for the moose. The frame is the camera at arrival, clamped to the district like the real camera; it must contain the whole signpost on the portrait phone. The magnifier shows the pixels the device would actually draw at the chosen render scale and DPR, enlarged 4x with no smoothing, so softness and edge fringe are visible.

Do the four moose parts assemble and move as one?

Drag a part to move it. Hold Shift and click on a part to set its pivot (drawn as a ring). Antlers and eye are children of the head; the head is a child of the body. Play the reactions and look for gaps opening at the neck and at the antler bases. Reduced motion freezes the idle breathing but still plays click reactions, as in ADR 0002.
