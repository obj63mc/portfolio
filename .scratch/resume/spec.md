principal full-stack role, styled with the site's type and colours, and printed by the browser to the PDF at `/resume.pdf`

## Decided (Joe, 2026-10-02)

- Route: `/resume`, a page with no scene or room, as the games are. The engine steps away while it is up, and its
  controls, the Sound toggle and the joystick, are hidden with the room's count (Joe, 2026-10-02).
- The copy is plain HTML in the page, not a data module or Markdown: easiest to add to and edit. Tests read the markup's
  shape, never its words.
- On screen, the night board the games use: night panel framed in ivory, gold headline, sky section headings, ivory copy,
  on a lighter night. In print, white paper and night ink on two Letter pages.
- The header: Joseph Madden, "Principal Full-Stack Engineer · Web Architect", St. Louis, MO, joe@barmadden.com,
  barmadden.com, GitHub and LinkedIn. No phone, no street address.
- The history is LinkedIn's: Chief Architect at Moosylvania from Aug 2013, Senior Web Developer there Aug 2011 to Aug 2013,
  Engineer III at Network Solutions (MonsterCommerce until the 2005 acquisition) May 2004 to Aug 2011; SLU 2001 to 2005.
- Numbers Joe gave: teams of one to ten developers, five to six websites a year, about 50 emails a year, the hosting of
  about 45 websites and applications for Gallo Spirits and Scandinavian Tobacco Group, every website of STG's North
  American cigar unit. Barefoot Wine was promotional landing pages, not the whole site. The site's own performance and
  test numbers stay off the resume.
- Every brand on the site may be named, Universal's titles included.
- The bar: Download PDF (`/resume.pdf`, a download) and Print; the round exit, to `/`, lands at the arrival point.
- The welcome sign's card links the page. A click to `/resume` counts as a resume contact, as the PDF's does.
