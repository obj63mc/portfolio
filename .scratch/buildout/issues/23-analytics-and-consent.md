# 23: Analytics: GA4 events, deferred loading, consent popover, GPC

**What to build:** In production a bundled `dataLayer` and `gtag()` queue exist from the start (never an inline script) and gtag.js is injected after the first frame on `requestIdleCallback` with a 3 s timeout, only when `PUBLIC_GA_ID` is set, which the Workers Builds build script passes through only when `WORKERS_CI_BRANCH` is `main`; with no id there is no script and no queue. Enhanced measurement is off; `page_view` is sent manually from `afterNavigate` on every pathname change including the first load, never for fragments. Events: `page_view` (scene path), `card_open` (`prop_id`, `scene`), `contact_click` (`method`), `cosmetic_earned` (`cosmetic_id`, first grant only), `gold_cursor`, `screen_play` (`title`, when the visitor's own click is accepted). Nothing else, and never the flag, socket id or any user id. Ad signals denied everywhere, Google Signals off, and a region-denied `consent default` for EEA, UK and CH as the backstop. A visitor in a `Europe/*`, Atlantic EU or `Africa/Ceuta` timezone with no stored choice sees a one-line bar ("Can I count visits with Google Analytics? No ads, no tracking elsewhere.") with equal Allow and No thanks buttons as a `popover` stacked above the Join card, operable before Join and not counting as Join; events queue in memory until a choice; Allow loads gtag and flushes; No thanks discards and gtag never loads; the choice persists through the rune module's `analytics` field. The analytics icon beside the Sound toggle reopens the bar with the current choice pressed; No thanks after gtag has loaded sends `consent update denied`, as does a denial from another tab. Global Privacy Control true means GA never loads and the bar shows both buttons disabled with a note; Do Not Track is ignored.

## Comments

### Built, 2026-09-30

- **Where the ID comes from.** `svelte.config.js` exports `measurementId(env)`: `PUBLIC_GA_ID` passes only when `WORKERS_CI_BRANCH` is `main`, so Previews, local builds and `npm run dev` have none (stricter than the noindex step, where a local build counts as production), and a `.env` file can't leak one in. It always sets the variable, empty if need be, so `$env/static/public` always exports it and the empty string lets Rollup drop the GA code.
- **Modules.** `src/lib/analytics/consent.ts` (pure): the timezone test, the load-time decision (`off` with no ID, `gpc`, a stored choice, else `asking` in Europe and `granted` elsewhere), the EEA/UK/CH region list, which link is which `contact_click` method, and the scene a path names. `src/lib/analytics/tracker.ts`: what reaches gtag, through an injected `gtag` and loader. It sets up once (ad signals denied, `analytics_storage` granted by default but denied for the region list, `send_page_view: false`, Signals and ad personalisation off), holds events while asking, flushes them after a `consent update granted` on Allow, drops them on No thanks, and sends `consent update denied` only if gtag was set up. `src/lib/analytics.svelte.ts` is the page glue. It builds the `dataLayer`/`gtag()` queue at hydration and injects gtag.js on `requestAnimationFrame` then `requestIdleCallback` (3 s timeout), or just after that frame where there's no idle callback (Safari). It exposes the six events and the bar's state, applies a choice made in another tab through the `storage` event, and counts contact links from document `click` and `auxclick`. `saved.svelte.ts` gains an `analytics` setter.
- **Hooks.** `page_view` from the layout's `afterNavigate`, only when the pathname changes, with `page_location` set to origin plus pathname, so there's no fragment or query. `card_open` in `Prop.svelte`'s `open()`, where every card opens. `cosmetic_earned` and `gold_cursor` in the engine's grant handler, which a grant from another tab never passes through. The engine's locked middle click also reports its link. `screen_play` is in `projector.ts`. The room never says who asked, so acceptance is `accepted()` in `net/screen.ts`: a new reel of the asked title arriving after the ask. That means a click dropped while a reel plays never counts, two visitors asking for the same title at the same moment both count, and an offline play counts.
- **The bar, and a correction to the spec's premise.** A popover shown after a modal dialog is *not* operable in Chrome. The HTML spec makes everything outside the topmost modal dialog inert, whatever its place in the top layer. A probe confirmed it: the click falls on the card's backdrop and Tab never reaches the popover. So `Consent.svelte` is rendered twice and shows in one place at a time. The first copy sits inside the Join card while the card is up, a popover over it that its modality leaves operable and that Tab reaches straight after Join. The second sits on the page after Join (the bar stays until a choice) and whenever the icon reopens it. Before the engine starts, the page copy shows. Each time the Join card opens, the bar is raised over it again. On the page, the cursor canvas is raised over the bar, so a locked cursor aiming at a button stays drawn. The page copy joins the engine's `CONTROLS` (push suppression within 40 px; on touch it's the finger's) and its touch exclusions. While Paused, it sits dimmed under the Paused card. Choosing returns focus to Join, or to the icon.
- **The icon.** In a build with GA it toggles the bar, with `aria-expanded`, the choice in force pressed and both buttons disabled plus a note under GPC. A build with no GA (every one but production's) keeps the icon disabled in its place, so Previews look like production and the ticket 10 phone smoke still finds its toggles.
- **Timezones.** `Europe/*`, `Atlantic/Canary`, `Azores`, `Madeira`, `Reykjavik` and `Africa/Ceuta`, plus **`Asia/Nicosia` and `Asia/Famagusta` (Cyprus, an EU member)**, an addition for Joe to confirm.
- **Tests.** `tests/analytics.test.ts`: timezones, the decision table (GPC over everything, no DNT input), contact methods from the scene data, the region list, the build gate, and the tracker against a fake gtag (queue, flush, discard, update after load, off and GPC silent). `tests/screen.test.ts`: `accepted`. `tests/build-output.test.ts`: a build with no ID has no gtag.js loader, no `dataLayer` and no bar, and a disabled icon. A build with one has all of them, its ID, and still no inline script. The check follows the same gate, so it holds on the Workers Builds `main` build too, and it was run both ways. `tests/consent.spec.ts` (Playwright): the bar over the Join card, reached by Tab and a real click before Join, Allow loading gtag and flushing `page_view`, the choice persisting over a reload, No thanks keeping gtag away through a card and the icon reopening it with No thanks pressed, joining without answering, a denial from a second tab sending the update, GPC, and another timezone loading with no bar, counting a scene once through a fragment, and sending `card_open` and `contact_click`.
- **How the smoke gets its test ID.** `playwright.config.ts` has a second web server. It builds the site as a `main` build with `PUBLIC_GA_ID=G-SMOKETEST` and `SMOKE_OUT=.smoke`, which `svelte.config.js` reads to put the adapter output and SvelteKit's `outDir` under `.smoke/` (ignored), so the site's own build and `vite preview`'s output are untouched. It serves that build on `SMOKE_PORT + 1`. The spec aborts and records every request to Google's hosts, so nothing is sent and gtag.js being asked for is observable. The smoke's pointer lock is a stand-in that refuses, so Join leaves the unlocked mouse.

### Hands-on, and Joe's GA settings

Only Chromium is installed for Playwright; WebKit and Firefox weren't run.

- [ ] Workers Builds: the `PUBLIC_GA_ID` build variable holds the property's `G-` ID. A `main` deploy's pages then load `gtag/js?id=G-…` after the first frame; a Preview's don't, and its icon is disabled.
- [ ] GA Admin → Data streams → the barmadden.com stream → **Enhanced measurement off** (page changes from history events, scrolls, outbound clicks, site search, video, file downloads, form interactions). Code can't turn it off; `send_page_view: false` only stops the automatic first page view.
- [ ] GA Admin → Data collection: **Google signals off**; granular location and device data off.
- [ ] GA Admin → Data retention: 2 months (the shortest), unless Joe wants longer.
- [ ] No Google Ads or other product links.
- [ ] Custom definitions: event-scoped dimensions for `prop_id`, `scene`, `method`, `cosmetic_id` and `title`, so reports show them.
- [ ] DebugView, with Tag Assistant on barmadden.com: `page_view` on load and on a hop and back, none on a signpost district link; `card_open`, `contact_click` (resume, email, LinkedIn, GitHub, Strava), `cosmetic_earned` on a first grant only, `gold_cursor` on the seventh, `screen_play` on an accepted poster click, each with only its listed params.
- [ ] A European timezone (the OS clock, or DevTools → Sensors): the bar over the Join card on a desktop, an iPhone (Safari) and Firefox, both buttons working before Join, focus back on Join. Under the real pointer lock, with the bar still up after Join, the drawn cursor is drawn over it and clicks its buttons.
- [ ] GPC on (Brave, or Firefox's setting): no request to googletagmanager.com, and the icon's bar is disabled with its note.
- [ ] Production CSP: gtag.js loads and `region1.google-analytics.com/g/collect` isn't blocked.
